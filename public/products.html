XColorRIP x64

A Windows RIP and print application for production printing. Built native for 64-bit, powered by Ghostscript 10.07 and a new image engine.

PlatformWindows 64-bit
EngineGhostscript 10.07, OpenCV-based image engine
ColorICC profiles, density curves, ink limits, 6-color Hi-Fi
OutputRoll or sheet, per job

Speed

Native 64-bit

Uses all the memory in your PC instead of being capped near 2 GB, so large files stay in memory.

Multi-threaded pipeline

RIP the next job while the current one prints.

Virtual Enhance for banners

Renders at a lower internal resolution and enlarges to the printer's native resolution. 3.6× faster on a 600×5000 test banner (SC4180H1), with no visible banding on gradients. Printer-dependent.

Color management

Consistent, repeatable color from density curve to ICC profile.

  • Density curves per ink, ink limits and ICC profiles, applied in one flow
  • 6-color Hi-Fi (CMYK + Orange + Green): RGB photos are converted through a proper RGB-to-CMYK step before the six-color transform, so shadows keep their depth
  • Works with ICC profiles from the tools you already use

White ink and DTF

  • One-pass white plus color on the Epson XP15010 and the L805 / L1800 series
  • Transparent PNG becomes clean white automatically
  • White underbase placed first, color on top, aligned in a single pass
